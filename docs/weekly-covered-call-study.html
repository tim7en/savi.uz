I · Is it realistic now?

Not with the present $10,000 account.

One standard covered call requires 100 shares. At the latest local closes, one fully covered SPY contract needs approximately $76,347 of SPY and one QQQ contract needs $70,632 of QQQ. A $10,000 account holds only about 13.1 SPY shares or 14.2 QQQ shares.

Do not solve the sizing problem with naked calls. XSP, call spreads, or broker-specific fractional option products are different strategies with basis, liquidity, settlement, and tail-risk differences. They should not be described as ordinary covered calls.

III · CAPE and volatility gates

Your OR rule is almost the same as always selling.

“High CAPE” is CAPE ≥25, “extreme” is CAPE >35, and “calm” means the prior-known 60-session VIX or VXN percentile is below 70%. Those definitions use only information available on the call-sale date. What the test says: calm volatility alone passed 296 SPY weeks and 284 QQQ weeks. It lifted the tested SPY endpoint by 1.98% and QQQ by 4.29% versus no calls. That is a historical attribution, not evidence that the threshold will persist out of sample.

Do not select the rule only because it won this sample. The result is sensitive to a handful of rebound weeks. A defensible live rule should be frozen before trading and evaluated on later data.

VII · Operating rule if tested live

Five delta, calm volatility, and partial coverage.

RegimeUse prior-known 60-session VIX/VXN percentile below 70%. Do not use CAPE ≥25 OR calm: it was active 99.2% of this sample.
EligibilitySell only against a fully paid, unleveraged 100-share index lot. Never count leveraged shares twice.
StrikeClosest listed weekly call near 5 delta; skip the week if bid, spread, or strike quality is poor.
SizeCover no more than 25–50% of eligible shares initially; keep the rest uncapped for rebounds.
PremiumCredit gross premium to Treasury, but separately reserve expected assignment/repurchase liquidity.
Stop conditionFreeze the rule for 52 trades; compare net option P&L and total portfolio return, not win rate or premium alone.

My conclusion: your regime-conditioned idea is more sensible than continuous overwriting. The cleanest candidate is calm-volatility-only, not CAPE-high OR calm, because the OR formulation scarcely filters. Even then, this is a small experimental Treasury sleeve—not the strategy engine. Ten- and twenty-delta weekly overwriting remain rejected by this sample.
